4. How Meal Scanning Works

When you submit a meal photograph or text description, Nomnom processes the request through its backend.

The current processing sequence is:

For AI analysis, Nomnom sends the meal photograph or the text description required for the request.

Nomnom does not intentionally include your email address, name or Nomnom account identity in the meal-analysis content sent to Anthropic.

However, meal photographs and descriptions may themselves contain identifying or sensitive information.

The absence of an account identifier does not necessarily make the submitted content anonymous.

Nomnom does not guarantee that AI-generated information is accurate.

  • The application submits the scan request to Nomnom's backend, hosted by Railway in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
  • The backend checks account authentication and applicable scan limits.
  • A meal photograph or text description is sent to Anthropic's AI service for analysis.
  • Anthropic's AI system returns estimated food identification and nutritional information.
  • The backend processes the response and returns the relevant results to the application.
  • Meal records and associated data are maintained through Supabase, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.

10. How Long We Keep Information

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as necessary for legitimate, disclosed purposes or required by applicable law.

The current architecture includes scheduled maintenance operations for account deletion and photo pruning, which run every six hours.

Some information may be retained longer where necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, the establishment or defence of legal claims, or other purposes permitted by law.

Third-party providers may maintain limited data in their own systems according to their agreements and applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, it will be deleted or anonymised in accordance with applicable law and the actual technical procedures.

  • Active account and authentication records: while the account remains active and as otherwise required by law.
  • Profile and body information: while the account remains active, or until you ask us to remove it.
  • Meal records, nutritional estimates and challenge progress: while the account remains active; removed with the account as described below.
  • Stored meal photographs: kept while the meal is among your three most recent days of logging, which is what the history screen shows. Once a newer day of logging exists, photographs from the oldest day are deleted within a few hours. The meal and its figures remain.
  • Temporary or queued uploads: a photograph taken offline stays on your device until it is sent. A photograph uploaded but never confirmed as a meal is deleted after four days.
  • Deleted-account data: when you delete your account it is scheduled for removal. Signing back in within 30 days cancels that. After 30 days your login, profile, meals, photographs and challenge records are permanently erased.
  • Server and security logs: up to 30 days.
  • Support emails and attachments: until the conversation is closed, then deleted within one year.
  • Payment and subscription records: for the life of the subscription and as required by applicable platform arrangements and legal recordkeeping requirements. Apple keeps its own purchase history under its own rules.
  • Backups: database backups kept by our hosting provider expire within 30 days.
