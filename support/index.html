Nomnom Support

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Contact us

Write to assistance.nomnom@gmail.com. We answer within two working days, in English, German or French.

To help us solve your problem quickly, please include:

  • The email address of your Nomnom account.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The app version, shown under You, then Other, then App version.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

Scanning meals

Point the camera at your meal or at a product barcode and take the photo. Nomnom estimates the calories and macros, and you confirm or adjust them before the meal is saved.

  • The free plan includes 2 meal scans per day. Premium includes 8. The counter resets at midnight in your local time.
  • Barcode scans use the product label and do not count against your scan quota.
  • If a scan fails or the estimate is wrong, use Describe it instead or Manual add and type the meal. A precise description with weights gives the most accurate figures.
  • Calorie and nutrient figures are estimates for your own information. They are not medical advice.

Nomnom, animations and frames

Nomnom reacts to what you ate yesterday. Feeding him after a scan plays an animation for that type of food. Challenges unlock more animations, and logging on consecutive days unlocks new frames for his card. Tap the question mark on the Today page for the walkthrough.

Premium subscription

Premium is sold through the App Store as a monthly or yearly subscription.

  • To cancel or change your plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription by itself.
  • To restore a purchase on a new phone, open You, then Subscription, then Restore purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your account and data

  • Change your details under You, then Account.
  • Delete your account under You, then Other, then Delete my account. Your data is removed within 30 days, and the deletion cannot be undone.
  • Meal photos are kept only while the meal is among your three most recent days of logging, then deleted. The meal and its figures stay in your history.
  • Nomnom works offline. Meals you log without a connection are sent when you are back online.
  • The Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use are available at nomnom-food.github.io.
